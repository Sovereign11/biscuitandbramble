Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026. Westhampton, New York, United States.

Biscuit & Bramble is a small shop that sells personalized pet keepsakes through Etsy. This policy explains what information we handle, why, and for how long. It covers three things: orders placed in our Etsy shop, our own software that schedules posts to Pinterest, and the social accounts where we share our designs.

The short version: we collect only what is needed to make and ship your order, we never sell or share your information for marketing, and the software we use to post to Pinterest touches only our own account.

1. Orders placed through Etsy

When you buy from us on Etsy, Etsy collects your name, shipping address, payment details and any personalization you type in. Etsy's handling of that information is governed by Etsy's Privacy Policy. We never see your payment details.

To make your order, we receive from Etsy:

  • Your name and shipping address, so the item can be shipped to you.
  • The personalization you entered, such as your pet's name, coat color or a year.
  • For photo-portrait items, the photos you upload of your pet.

We use this information only to make and ship your order and to answer questions about it. Shipping details are passed to our production partner, Printify (North Las Vegas, Nevada), which prints and ships the item. Printify's handling of that information is governed by Printify's Privacy Policy.

2. Pet photos for portrait items

If you order a portrait made from your pet's photo, we use the photo only to create your portrait. The photo is used as a reference by the image tools that draw the illustration, and the illustration is reviewed by a person before it is printed. Your photo is not used to train any system, is not posted anywhere, and is not used for any other order.

Photos are kept for 60 days after your order ships, in case a reprint is needed, and are then deleted.

3. Our Pinterest scheduling software

We run a small private program, used only by the shop owner, that posts pins about our own products to our own Pinterest boards. If you reached this page from Pinterest's developer portal, this is the section that describes that program.

  • What it does: it creates pins on boards belonging to the Biscuit & Bramble Pinterest account, using images and text the shop owner wrote and approved.
  • Whose data it uses: only the shop's own account. It does not read, store or analyze information about any other Pinterest user, and it has no users other than the shop owner.
  • What it stores: an access token for the shop's own account, kept on the owner's computer, and a record of which pins it has created. Nothing else.
  • What it never does: it does not follow, message or interact with other accounts, and it does not collect data from Pinterest for any purpose other than confirming its own pins were created.

4. Social media

We share our designs on Pinterest and TikTok. If you interact with those posts, that interaction is governed by each platform's own privacy policy. We do not collect information from those platforms about the people who view or interact with our posts.

5. Information we do not collect

We do not run a website that sets cookies or tracks visitors, we do not keep a mailing list, and we do not buy, sell or trade personal information. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

6. How long we keep information

  • Order details (name, address, personalization): as long as Etsy keeps the order in our shop records, which we rely on for returns and reprints.
  • Pet photos: 60 days after shipping, then deleted.
  • Pinterest access token: until it expires or the program is retired.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to delete your pet's photos sooner than 60 days, or to tell you what information we hold about your order. Message us through our Etsy shop and we will respond within a few days. Requests about information held by Etsy, Printify, Pinterest or TikTok should go to those companies directly.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.
